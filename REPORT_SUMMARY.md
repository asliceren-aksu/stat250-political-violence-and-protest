# Report summary

The study covers 22,110 events recorded in Türkiye during 2016–2023, of which 2,224 had at least one reported fatality. Protests made up the majority of recorded events. The group examined differences in reported fatality status, source counts, and population context across event types.

Violent event categories had a higher reported fatal event rate. Fatal events had slightly higher mean source counts, although the report notes the same median for both groups. The population comparison remained statistically significant after excluding zero log-population values. In the exploratory regression, the violent-event indicator showed the strongest positive association highlighted in the report.

These findings describe recorded events. Reporting coverage can vary by location, event type, and period. A recorded zero denotes zero reported fatalities, and the observational analysis cannot establish causation.

Source: [ACLED](https://acleddata.com/), as processed in the original METU STAT 250 group project.
